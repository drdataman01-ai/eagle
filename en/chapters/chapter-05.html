Chapter 05 of 12

The Wind and the Sea Were Still

My Master and His disciples got into a boat to travel to another village. That day, the wind and the waves had a little quarrel.

They seemed to be arguing over a question that mattered greatly to them: Who was moving the clouds across the sky—the wind or the waves? Perhaps the afternoon had been too quiet and they were simply bored. The wind blew again and again from the mountaintops toward the sea, while the waves refused to be outdone. One wave after another rose up and rushed toward the wind.

My eagle companions came flying over as well. We spread our wings and soared above the wind and the waves.

But gradually, the sky began to change. The bright sky slowly disappeared beneath the clouds. The golden light of the setting sun faded little by little. The sky turned gray. Then dark gray. In the distance, the clouds piled higher and higher, like mountains of darkness rising upon the horizon. The wind began to blow harder.

Then, suddenly, wave after wave rose upon the sea. The wind roared. The whole sea seemed to turn upside down. The birds along the shore became frightened. The deer on the hills scattered. Even the fish beneath the water fled in confusion.

The little game between the wind and the waves continued, again and again, while the boat was tossed violently upon the sea. Some of my Master's disciples were experienced fishermen. They knew the sea. They knew boats. They knew storms. Yet even they became afraid. The fishermen cried out,

“We are going to drown!”

One of them went into the boat and woke my Master.

“Lord, save us!”

My Master awoke. He looked at them and said,

“Why are you so afraid? Do you still have no faith?”

Then He stood and rebuked the wind and the waves.

“Quiet! Be still!”

Immediately, the wind stopped.

The waves became still.

The whole sea fell silent. It was like the moment a teacher walks into a classroom and all the children suddenly stop talking.

The fishermen stared at Him in amazement.

“Who is this?” they asked.

“Even the wind and the waves obey Him!”

The little quarrel between the wind and the waves had certainly made the afternoon more interesting.

But I was even more proud of my Master. He had shown them that He was Lord over all creation. He spoke only a few words, and the wind and the waves became still.

For us eagles, that was enough. We saw the wind and the waves obey Him, and we knew that He was Lord over all things. But you human beings are very different. You seem to need Him to show you before you are willing to believe.

Human beings really are strange.

When a storm comes, we simply fly away.
