Preface

Why This Story Took Flight

Some stories have been told so many times. The birth of Jesus. His ministry. His healing of the sick. His miracles. His crucifixion. His resurrection from the dead. Perhaps you already know these stories almost too well.

Sometimes I wonder: If I had truly stood there in those days, watching all these things unfold before my eyes, what would I have seen? A man? A teacher? A prophet who performed miracles? That question remained with me throughout the writing of this story.

The first version of this story was written in 2021, during the global pandemic. Those were days unlike any we had known before. Many people were forced to remain at home, and the world seemed to grow strangely quiet. During that time, I too stayed at home and began to write this story.

There are scenes that still move me, especially the seventh chapter, “My Master Was Crucified.” Perhaps what remains is not merely the writing. Perhaps what remains is the One I was thinking about in those quiet days— Jesus Christ.

The eagle in this story flies through the skies of Galilee, looking down upon valleys, lakes, villages, and crowds of people. It sees the crowds gathering on the hillsides. It sees the disciples following Him. It sees hostility slowly rising among the people. It sees the Roman soldiers. And finally, it sees the cross.

Yet the eagle sees more than what is happening on the earth. In its eyes, there is a light upon Jesus unlike anything it has ever seen. It is not the light of the sun. It is not the golden light of sunset falling upon the mountain walls. It is a different kind of light— the light of glory.

And the eagle keeps asking:

“Who is He?”

It sees Him calm the storm.

It sees Him feed five thousand people.

It sees Him nailed to the cross.

It sees Him rise again on the third day.

It sees grace entering the world of men.

It sees the One who created heaven and earth enter the very world He created. And this eagle becomes the eagle who carries Stephen before the throne of God.

This eagle also becomes the eagle who will one day cry out from the heavens, calling humanity to repentance when the final judgment comes.

So I invite you to fly with this young eagle.

From the valleys to the lake.

From the crowds to the cross.

From death to resurrection.

May you fly with this eagle

and see the glory of Christ.

Author's Note

Let Us Fly Together

This story began in 2021. During the global pandemic, while the world grew quiet and I remained at home, I wrote of a young eagle—and what it saw when it lifted its eyes toward Jesus.

Years later, I returned to these pages and found the story still moved me, as though the eagle had been waiting for the wind to rise again. So I have set it once more into the open sky.

May its wings lift your eyes upward, and may you, too, behold the One it came to see. Come, then.

Let us fly together.

Chris Kuo
Author, The Eyes of the Eagle: See the Glory
